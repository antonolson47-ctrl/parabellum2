# PARABELLUM 2: Something Old, Something Undead
18+ mobile zombie FPS (iPhone landscape first) — prequel to PARABELLUM. Shayla & Kennedy are engaged and fight across 7 scenes to collect something old, borrowed, new and blue.

Play: https://antonolson47-ctrl.github.io/parabellum2/  ·  Chapter select on the title (add `?chapters=all` to unlock every scene).

Scenes: 1 Razorcrack Stadium (night) · 2 Arkadelphia Main St → 67 Grill, Mom's brooch · 3 Fayetteville Square, Kayleigh's lucky girdle (+10% armor) · 4 Freddo's on I-30 · 5 Colorado Springs jewelry store, the necklace · 6 Garden of the Gods, 12 blue columbines vs zombie trolls · 7 Gold mine, minigun + rocket launcher, gold bars, boss Old Man Grubstake, ending in the sunlight.

Teamwork: Kennedy always at your side · mutual revive · 💞 couple combos · cover fire while you reload · tandem finisher (he grabs, you headshot) · partner meter → TEAM UP slow-mo triple damage.

Build: `npm i && ./build.sh` (dist/) · `./make_pages.sh` assembles ../parabellum2-pages. All music/sfx are procedural & original.
